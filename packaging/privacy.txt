What leaves your PC

Tycho runs on your PC. The Tycho project runs no servers of its own and
receives nothing about you or how you use it. There are no analytics.

- Your voice never leaves your PC: speech is turned into text here.
- What you say, as text, goes to Claude (Anthropic, or OpenRouter if you
  choose it), and only once you store a key for it. With it go your
  keybinds, commands, memory notes and the last few requests.
- What Tycho says, as text, goes to ElevenLabs, only once you store a key.
- Web search sends a short query to OpenRouter and Perplexity, or to
  Anthropic. Off with web_search = false.
- Questions about trade, items and blueprints send the names you ask about
  to UEX Corp and the Star Citizen Wiki API. Their ship lists, and the speech
  model from Hugging Face, are downloaded to your PC.
- GitHub is asked whether a newer Tycho is out. Off with
  check_for_updates = false.
- Off until you say yes, in setup or later:
  crash reports to Sentry (crash_reports), a picture of the game window
  when you ask Tycho to look (look_at_screen), and keeping a history of
  your conversations on your PC (keep_history).

Your settings are in %APPDATA%\tycho\settings.toml, and in Tycho's window
under Settings, Privacy. Your API keys stay in Windows Credential Manager.

The full privacy policy, service by service, with each service's own
policy: https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
