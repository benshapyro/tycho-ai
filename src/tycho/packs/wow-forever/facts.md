Game: World of Warcraft: Forever, Blizzard's version of the original Azeroth that grows from level 1 to 60. It came out on 4 November 2026 and is its own game, apart from modern World of Warcraft (the Midnight expansion) and from WoW Classic: nothing from those applies unless the player asks about them. Latest hotfixes: 11 November 2026. Checked 13 November 2026.

- The level cap is 60, and there's no level scaling: zones and dungeons keep their own levels.
- The world is the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor, with four new zones: Zephras Isle (the Skyborne starting zone, levels 1 to 12), the Riverglades (mid-30s to mid-40s), Shen'dralas (a hidden place between Mulgore and Desolace) and Mount Hyjal (the level-60 zone).
- The classes are the original nine: Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Shaman, Mage, Warlock and Druid. Every class can be played on both factions, though not by every race.
- New race: the Skyborne, who can join either faction. Both sides can be Warriors, Hunters, Rogues or Druids; Horde Skyborne can also be Shamans and Alliance Skyborne Mages. No Skyborne Paladins.
- Nine new five-player dungeons run from level 13 to 60, among them Hall of Thanes (the first), Ruins of Lordaeron, the Excavation Site, the City of Dalaran, the Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold and Shaper's Terrace (the last).
- Raids: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair open on 9 December 2026, for level-60 characters.
- The Legacy System is new: Legacy Points are earned across the whole account, and each character spends them on three trees of perks, Professions, Adventure and Resourcefulness, apart from its class talents.
- No flying: travel is on foot, by ground mount (riding from level 40, faster riding at 60), by boat and by flight paths.
- There are no realms, only rulesets: Normal, PvP and Roleplay, with Hardcore (one life) coming later.
- Addons are limited as in modern WoW: they can show information but not decide for the player, and some combat addons don't work.
