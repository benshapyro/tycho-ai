Expansion: Midnight, released 2 March 2026. Patch 12.1.5 is live and 12.1.7 is in testing (from Blizzard's hotfix notes, 6 November 2026). Checked 12 November 2026.

- The level cap in Midnight is 90.
- Midnight is set in Quel'Thalas: Silvermoon City and Eversong Woods (both remade), Zul'Aman, Harandar and the Voidstorm.
- New in Midnight: the Haranir, a playable race from Harandar; a third Demon Hunter spec, Devourer, which fights at range; and player housing.
- Addons can no longer read some combat information during combat, so some combat addons stopped working or changed in Midnight.
