# Changelog

What changed in each version of Tycho, for players. The newest version is first.

Each version's full notes, with examples, are on its [GitHub release](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/releases). The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/): while Tycho is 0.x, the second number goes up for new features and the third for fixes only.

**Adding to it:** a PR that changes something a player can notice adds a line under **Unreleased**, in plain words, with its PR or issue. Fixes to work that hasn't been released yet, and work behind the scenes, don't need a line. The release moves **Unreleased** under the new version's number and date (see the `release` skill).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Say "plot a route to Levski" (or "set a course for Area 18") from your pilot's seat, and Tycho opens the star map, searches for the place, plots the route and closes the map again. It says "Route set to Levski." only once the game's log shows the route. If a route is already set, Tycho clears it first and says so. The map only searches the system you're in, and Tycho tells you if it can't find the place, if your quantum fuel may be low, or if you're not seated. It makes at most two mouse clicks, both on the map, and sets NAV mode first so a click can't fire a weapon. Place names come from UEX's list. Other ways of asking ("take us to Crusader") and misheard ones ("plotta route to Lewski") work too, and Tycho only says a route is set once it is. When it only guessed the place ("Dlevsky"), it asks "Plot a route to Levski?" first: say "confirm". Ask for a place "in the next system" and Tycho says the map only plots routes in the system you're in. Tycho waits for the map to finish opening before it clicks the search box, and pauses before typing. A place name Tycho doesn't know ("plot a route to Lipsky") goes to the brain, which can tell it's Levski. When Enter opens the place's own panel instead of a list, Tycho clicks the place's name there before plotting, and the same when Enter leaves the place at the top of the map's RECENT list. Tycho reads only the left half of the screen while it plots, where the map's search panel is, so each check is quicker and misreads fewer letters on wide screens. "Plot a route to Pyro Gateway" works too: of two places with the same name, Tycho picks the one in your system and types the name the map knows. "Ariel" finds the moon Arial, and asking with the star map already open works too. Tycho waits while the map works out the fuel to the place before it plots, and names with a letter on one of your keybinds ("Aberdeen") can be typed once the search box has the keys. Tycho clicks the place itself in the search results when it can, so "MicroTech" is the planet and not a depot of the same name, and it never picks a place from another system. ([#588](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/588), [#647](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/647), [#650](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/650), [#661](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/661), [#682](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/682))
- You can have two push-to-talk buttons, say a mouse button and a button on your joystick, and hold whichever is closer. Add the second in setup (Talk and listen) or in Settings, under Game and controls, and remove it there too. In a terminal: `tycho bind --second` and `tycho bind --remove-second`. ([#621](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/621))
- Tycho keeps a list of the Star Citizen blueprints you own, on your PC. It reads your older game logs once, then adds each new blueprint a mission gives you. Ask "do I have the Omnisky III blueprint?" or "which blueprints have I got?". Blueprint answers also say when you already own one. Add one yourself with "I've got the Killshot Rifle blueprint". See and delete them on the Memory page, or say "forget the ... blueprint". With memory off, nothing is kept or used. (#598)
- Tycho can tell you when a mission gives you a blueprint ("New blueprint: Monde Helmet Hiro."). Turn on "Blueprints received" under speaking up, or say "tell me about blueprints". (#598)
- Ask Tycho where a crafting blueprint comes from, or what a mission gives: "where do I get the Omnisky III blueprint?" names the mission, who gives it, the system and the standing you need, and says it's one of several the mission picks from ("one of 10"). "What blueprints can I get from Vaughn?" lists them. It's from the current patch's game files, through the Star Citizen Wiki. ([#596](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/596))
- "How do I make a P4-AR?" now gets the whole answer: the recipe, then where to mine each material, looked up together. Recipes also say what each material's quality improves ("better Iron hits harder and fires faster") and any minimum quality. ([#597](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/597))
- Tycho can keep a history of what you and it said, if you say yes: setup asks once (and asks players who set up before, on their next start), and it's off unless you do. Then ask "what was yesterday's route?" or "what did you tell me about Hurston yesterday?" and Tycho reads it back. Only your words, Tycho's words and the names of the actions it pressed are kept, with the time: never your voice, a picture or your keys. It stays on your PC, one file per play session, and is deleted after 30 days. Old talk is never a reason to press anything: "do yesterday's route again" gets told, not done, and you ask for each press yourself. See, search and delete it on the new History page; turn it off in Settings, under Privacy, or say "stop keeping a history". ([#484](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/484))
- Tycho remembers you. Say "remember I trade out of Area18" or "keep in mind I like short answers", or say yes when Tycho offers, and later answers use it. Notes are for this game or for every game, at most 50 of each, each with the day it was saved. "Forget that" or "forget that I fly a Cutlass" removes one, and "what do you remember about me?" reads them back. Tycho never keeps a note about pressing or confirming ("eject without asking"): the safety checks stay. The notes are plain files on your PC (`memory.toml`) and go to the brain with each request; see and delete them on the new Memory page in Tycho's window, which also shows your ships and what Tycho calls you. Turn memory off in Settings, under Privacy, or by voice. ([#483](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/483))
- Tycho now notes facts about how you play as you mention them, without you saying "remember": say "I'm in my Cutlass" and it answers "Noted, you use a Cutlass." It only keeps your own words, never something it looked up, and "forget that" undoes it. Anything about you outside the game, and notes for every game, still need "remember" or a yes. ([#483](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/483))
- Tycho can fill in your ships from your hangar: export your hangar on ccugame.app, then in Settings, under Game and controls, choose Import next to "Your ships" and pick `ships.csv` or `fleetview.json`. Tycho shows what it will add, and any names it couldn't match, before it saves. Importing again later swaps in the new list and keeps the ships you told Tycho about yourself. Only the ship names are kept: never your account, pledge numbers or what you paid, and nothing leaves your PC. Agents can run `tycho fleet import <file>`. ([#556](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/556))
- Tycho turns the game's sound down while it speaks, so you can hear it over engines and combat, then fades it back up. It's the game's own slider in the Windows volume mixer, nothing in the game. Only the game: other programs, like Discord, are left alone, and each of the game's sliders goes back to its own level. On by default, to 30% of where you left it: change it in Settings, under Voice, or say "stop turning the game down" or "game at 20% while you talk". If you move the game's slider yourself while Tycho speaks, yours wins. ([#570](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/570))

A comparison no longer repeats its two names in the title, and still reads right when the brain packs both figures into one field. A list whose values are all the same no longer lights the first row as "best". The "Last answer" card doesn't say "Last answer" twice, and a question card now adds "Wait, and nothing is pressed." A comparison says a unit both figures share once, beside what's compared ("BUY IN GAME · AUEC"), so big figures no longer crowd the middle. A list's bars sit clear of the names, so they don't read as underlines. Spaced capital letters are measured as they're drawn, so a title or label can't run a few pixels past its room. ([#688](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/688))

A long title wraps onto a second line instead of running off the card's edge.

- Tycho sometimes answered a question with the single word "unused" (once, for "which blueprints have I got?"). That was the brain filling in a blank. It is never said now: Tycho takes the brain's real answer instead. ([#666](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/666))

- `crash.txt` in Tycho's logs folder now starts with a note saying Tycho is running and that a Windows exception written there may have been handled, so a handled one no longer reads like a crash. ([#582](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/582))
- Right after a web search or a card, a follow-up question no longer gets Tycho saying it didn't search, and searching the same thing again: it now remembers what it looked up and showed for half a minute, as it does what it said. ([#564](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/564))

- In World of Warcraft: Forever, the brain no longer shows ship, fleet or loadout cards, which Settings had no switch to hide. Every card it can show now has a switch in Settings, under The Tile. ([#528](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/528))

## [0.10.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- Tycho can look at the game window when you ask: say "take a look" (or "what am I looking at?"), or say "yes" when Tycho asks "Want me to take a look?". It's off until you say yes, in setup's privacy step or in Settings, and players who set Tycho up before are asked once. Only the game's own window is captured, you hear a camera click each time, and the picture goes to the brain with your question and nowhere else: it's never saved or logged. Saying "stop looking at my screen" turns it off, and a look never presses a key. ([#31](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/31))
- With one card at a time, the card that gives way now stays as a thin strip for a minute, and so does the one before it. Say "go back a card" or "last card" to bring one back, and "next card" to go forward again. "Hide the tile" and "keep the tile there" also work now. ([#491](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/491))
- Two new Tile cards: a loadout (what a ship is fitted with, by slot and size, for questions like "what's the best loadout for my M80?") and a comparison (two ships side by side, figure against figure). Lists of numbers now show a bar for each, so the biggest and smallest stand out at a glance. ([#491](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/491))
- Tycho can speak up without being asked, about a few things the game's log records: a quantum jump ending, entering or leaving monitored space, a new jurisdiction, an armistice zone, and private property. It's off until you pick which kinds in Settings, under Voice ("Speaking up"), or ask ("tell me when a quantum jump ends"). Each is one short line, at most one every 30 seconds, never while you or Tycho are talking, and push-to-talk stops it. Say "stop the callouts" to turn them all off. Without a voice, the line shows on the Tile. ([#4](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/4))

### Changed
- Tycho gets the brain ready while you hold push-to-talk, so its answer starts a little sooner, and the first question after a break of an hour or more is no longer about 3 seconds slower. ([#21](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/21))
- When Tycho can only reach an action through a toggle (say, "open the doors" with only Open/Close Doors bound), it now says "Toggled Open/Close Doors. I can't see which way it went." instead of a line that claims the doors are open. If it pressed something else as well, the rest of its line stays and this follows it. Give the action its own key and the line goes away. Actions the game announces itself, like the landing gear, stay quiet as before. ([#29](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/29), [#537](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/537))
- Web search is much faster and cheaper with an OpenRouter key: Tycho searches through it, with Perplexity's search, in about 1.5 seconds for about half a cent, instead of about 10 seconds and 3 cents. That's whichever runs the brain. With only an OpenRouter key, web search now works at all; with only an Anthropic key, nothing changes. If you have both and OpenRouter refuses your key (revoked, or out of credit), Tycho searches with your Anthropic key instead. ([#365](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/365))
- A slow web search gives up sooner (18 s instead of 25 s), so Tycho always has time to answer from it rather than running out of time just after it finishes. Searches took 15 s at most when measured. ([#365](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/365))

### Fixed
- After you join a server, Tycho no longer counts a quantum route you plotted before as still set: plot it again before asking to engage quantum travel. ([#30](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/30))

## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- New commands for remote turrets, such as the Idris's: "enter remote turret" (or "enter remote turret two" or "three") and "exit remote turret". The game has no key for entering one by default, so bind Enter Remote Turret 1, 2 and 3 in the game's keybinds first. ([#356](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/356))
- Ask Tycho which key does something ("how do I fire the guns?", "what's my strafe key?") and it names your own key, from your keybinds. ([#357](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/357))

### Fixed
- A card the brain left empty no longer ends in the not-done sound: Tycho says its line without the card. ([#443](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/443))
- "Open hangar" heard as "open hangers", and "close doors" heard as "closed doors", now work instantly instead of waiting for the brain. ([#358](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/358))
- Asked about "the address" when you said the Idris, Tycho now answers about the Idris instead of asking which ship you mean. A real address is still left alone: the brain is only told what the word may be. ([#509](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/509))
- Tycho no longer hears an everyday word such as "hunger" as a game word that only sounds like it ("hangar"). ([#505](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/505))
- Setup's Try it is safer. What you say there is tried, not pressed, even if you switch to the game while Tycho is still working it out. If the window misses your switch to the game and Try it stays on, what you say there plays the not-done sound and nothing is pressed, rather than a done sound for keys that never reached the game. After setting up a new game you picked in Settings, the window opens on Try it, as after a first run. Actions with no name in the game's files now read as words ("Toggle landing system"), not as raw ids, and a follow-up question no longer takes something you only tried as done. ([#467](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/467), [#465](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/465))
- Tycho no longer says it "stopped unexpectedly last time" when you ended it from Task Manager after the window hit a harmless Windows error that it recovered from. ([#474](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/474))
- A notice that comes while Tycho says "Let me check." during a look-up is now said before the answer, and the filler line isn't cut off. ([#440](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/440))
- Names and quotes in Greek or Cyrillic on the Tile now draw in Windows' own fonts. Since 0.8's bundled fonts they drew as boxes. ([#458](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/458))
- With speech recognition on the processor (the default), Tycho now uses at most as many threads as your PC has cores, so a 4-core laptop keeps more for the game. If the new speech model couldn't download after an update, Tycho starts on the one you already have instead of not starting. `tycho gpu install` now says how to move speech recognition to the graphics card, and `tycho check` says when the old 2.5 GB model is no longer used and can be deleted. ([#480](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/480))

### Changed
- Answers with more than a few numbers, such as a trade route or a loadout, now go on the Tile more often, with one sentence spoken. Asking to see "something interesting" on the Tile gets a card, not a question back. ([#490](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/490))
- A stray word that isn't a request, or one misheard, gets "Copy." instead of a question back, and "is that you?" gets a few words. A vague "the usual" gets a question, never a guess. ([#359](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/359), [#495](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/495))
- With key actions off, Tycho now knows every key you've bound, steering, strafing and emotes included, and says when one is held ("Boost (Hold)"). Asked for your strafe key, it names it instead of saying there's none. ([#437](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/437))
- In setup, skipping the Keys step in Star Citizen now shows it as skipped, not as still to do: a key is optional there, since the built-in phrases work without one. With key actions off it still shows as to do, and says why: the built-in phrases press nothing then. At the window's smallest size, Next sits fully in view, and in World of Warcraft: Forever, What to say now shows any problem with your own commands file. ([#448](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/448))
- **Quit** is now at the bottom of the left side of Tycho's window, so it's easy to find. It's still under Settings, Updates, and on Tycho's icon by the clock. ([#486](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/486))
- With the brain through OpenRouter, Tycho now tells OpenRouter never to use a host that keeps or trains on what you say, whatever your OpenRouter account's privacy settings are. ([#488](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/488))
- `tycho check`'s focus test no longer says "(another program was)" when it can't tell which program was in front; it names the program only when it knows. ([#430](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/430))

## [0.8.0] - 2026-11-22

### Added
- The Tile now shows cards over World of Warcraft: Forever too, in gold, for lists, steps and routes that are easier to read than hear. Setup tells you to set Display Mode to Windowed (Fullscreen), which the Tile needs. ([#381](https://github.com/benshapyro/tycho-ai/issues/381))
